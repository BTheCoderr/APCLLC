# APC LLC Website Roadmap

This roadmap describes direction, not guaranteed dates.

## Shipped

- production custom-domain site at apcllc.co
- mobile-first service and conversion UX
- quote/contact workflows
- service-oriented route structure
- sitemap/robots support
- Netlify production delivery

## Improving

- form-delivery reliability and confirmation states
- local SEO/service-page depth
- accessibility and mobile conversion polish
- image/performance optimization
- operational analytics without adding unnecessary complexity

## Exploring

- lightweight quote status/admin workflow
- richer service-area content
- customer proof/testimonial presentation

## Product boundary

The site should stay fast and conversion-focused. Avoid turning a small owner-operated logistics website into an oversized freight platform.
